## Fuentes (dato confirmado en 2 fuentes, revisadas el 17 nov 2026)
- GamesRadar, "The Making Of... Left 4 Dead": el Director controla "where and when zombies, mobs and special infected spawn" y "the audio cues and weapon/item drops", "monitoring players' actions and emotional responses": "how agitated they seem, how accurate their shooting is, how well they are co-operating". https://gamesradar.com/making-left-4-dead
- Wikipedia, "Left 4 Dead": el Director "tracks each player's current situation" y "will add or remove items and infected in response"; "places enemies and items in varying positions and quantities"; busca "a dynamic experience". Lanzamiento: 18 nov 2008 (Windows y Xbox 360). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_4_Dead
- Game Developer, "The discomfort zone: the hidden potential of Valve's AI Director": usa "a calculated 'stress level' for each player" para ajustar "the placement of items players find". https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-discomfort-zone-the-hidden-potential-of-valve-s-ai-director
- No decir que "te ayuda cuando vas mal" ni que hay "momentos de calma": ninguna de estas fuentes lo describe así. El porqué ("que ninguna partida sea igual") se apoya en "dynamic experience" (Wikipedia) y "a dramatically different experience" (Game Developer).
