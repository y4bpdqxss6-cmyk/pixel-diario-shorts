## Fuentes (dato confirmado en 2 fuentes, revisadas el 19 nov 2026)
- Super Mario Wiki, "Super Guide Block": aparece "when a player has lost a life eight times in a row on one level", en un jugador; la computadora controla a Luigi "to clear the stage in place of the player"; puedes retomar el control; "it does not count as an actual clear. Items collected do not count as well." Primera aparición: New Super Mario Bros. Wii (2009). https://www.mariowiki.com/Super_Guide_Block
- Guinness World Records, "First use of computer-controlled 'help' feature in a 2D platform game": New Super Mario Bros. Wii (Nintendo, 2009); "can be activated if a player dies eight times on a single level"; "the player is guided to complete a level they are struggling with"; idea de Miyamoto. https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/98933-first-use-of-computer-controlled-%e2%80%9chelp%e2%80%9d-feature-in-a-2d-platform-game
- Destructoid (avance de 2009): "When a player dies eight times on one of the game's 80 stages… a green 'Super Guide' block"; puedes tomar el control "at any time"; Miyamoto: no todos tienen "the skill set (or the patience) to complete every world. But he sure wants them to." https://vip-develop.destructoid.com/?p=58072
- No decir que da monedas estrella ni que funciona igual en otros Mario: en New Super Mario Bros. U bastan 5 vidas (Super Mario Wiki). La voz dice "la consola juega por ti" (Luigi solo en notas y etiquetas, para no dibujarlo).
