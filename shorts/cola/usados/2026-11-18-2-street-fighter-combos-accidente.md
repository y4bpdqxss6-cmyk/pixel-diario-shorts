## Fuentes (dato confirmado en 2 fuentes, revisadas el 17 nov 2026)
- Wikipedia, "Street Fighter II": el sistema de combos "came about by accident"; el productor Noritaka Funamizu lo notó al revisar errores ("I noticed something strange, curious") y "we decided to leave the feature as a hidden one". Lanzamiento: 1991. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_Fighter_II
- Game Developer, "Street Fighter II designer opens up about the 'cancelling bug'": Akira Nishitani: "we discovered a side effect: It became possible to execute specials after hitting with normals"; "it didn't seem to cause any bugs, so we decided it could be a feature"; "It wasn't something we anticipated". https://gamedeveloper.com/business/-i-street-fighter-ii-i-designer-opens-up-about-the-cancelling-bug-
- GGRecon, "How Street Fighter 2 Accidentally Invented Combos": "one of the creators noticed he could perform a regular attack, then follow up with a special attack"; primero quisieron arreglarlo; "Street Fighter 2 was much more fun with the combo glitch left in". https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/how-street-fighter-2-accidentally-invented-combos/
- Nishitani dice que no lo llamaría "bug", por eso el guion dice "accidente" y no "error de programación". No mencionar la etapa del coche (solo una fuente) ni nombrar quién lo descubrió (las fuentes no coinciden).
