## Fuentes (dato confirmado en 2 fuentes, revisadas el 19 nov 2026)
- Nintendo Life, "Early Donkey Kong design document from Miyamoto showcases Popeye origin" (ene. 2023): Donkey Kong "started life as a Popeye the Sailor Man project, featuring the main man himself alongside Olive Oyl and antagonist Bluto"; "Nintendo had failed to secure the license"; muestra el documento de diseño de Miyamoto con los sprites; arcade de 1981. https://nintendolife.com/news/2023/01/early-donkey-kong-design-document-from-miyamoto-showcases-popeye-origin
- Thumbsticks, "Popeye cartoon inspired Nintendo's Donkey Kong" (sep. 2022): "the deal to use the licence fell through", y Popeye, Bluto y Olive Oyl pasaron a ser "Jumpman (the original name for Mario), Donkey Kong, and Pauline"; Popeye "finally starred in his own game a year later"; cita de Miyamoto en Iwata Asks (2009). https://www.thumbsticks.com/popeye-cartoon-inspired-nintendo-donkey-kong/
- Nombres en español latino: Olivia (Olive Oyl) y Brutus (Bluto). No decir que el martillo reemplaza a la espinaca: Thumbsticks solo lo llama "antecedente".
- Nintendo Life lo cuenta como "as the tale goes"; las dos fuentes coinciden en la licencia, así que se mantiene.
